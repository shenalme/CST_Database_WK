9. Calculations

Material  consumed kg = mass × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)  →  kg CO₂e = consumed × factor per kg  |  MJ = consumed × MJ per kg
Operation energy  kWh = hours × kW × utilisation ÷ parts per cycle
Operation carbon  kg CO₂e = kWh × electricity factor + process mass (kg) × process factor (kg CO₂e per kg)
Operation energy demand  MJ = kWh × 3.6
Design total  = materials + manufacturing operations + assembly & finishing operations
Live  kWh = sum of meter readings for the operation (design kWh if none)  →  variance % = (live − design) ÷ design × 100  →  High above the alert %, Watch above half of it
Live total  = materials + Σ (live kWh × electricity factor + process carbon)
